Why this is the airline problem

A stay-night is one unit of perishable inventory with a 60-day booking window — exactly an airline seat. The optimal policy solves V(t) = maxp q(p,t)·p + (1−q(p,t))·V(t−1), the single-unit RM recursion. The historical host sold out (100% occupancy) — which looks healthy and is the signature of underpricing; the optimizer deliberately trades occupancy (81%) for rate ($208 vs $130), the core RM insight. Demand model: Poisson arrivals (Fourier seasonality × weekend × lead-time curve) with lognormal willingness-to-pay fitted by censored maximum likelihood (σ̂ = 0.34, truth 0.32).
